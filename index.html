01 Standing Committee on Finance Meeting

The parliamentary committee is convening to review the quarterly economic indicators, focusing on capital expenditure, inflation rates, and the impact of the latest tax regime adjustments on the middle class.

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology is introducing a new bill aimed at bolstering cybersecurity measures and standardizing digital public infrastructure for municipal governance.

Members of Parliament are discussing the accelerated integration of 3kW solar systems in urban households and the expansion of net-metering subsidies across states to meet sustainable energy goals.
